FOREVER FIT CLUB RULES AND REGULATIONS

CLUB RULES

General Club Use

  • All members must present their membership card upon entry
  • Members are responsible for their personal belongings
  • Lockers are available for daily use - please remove items at end of day
  • No smoking, vaping, or alcohol consumption on premises
  • Respectful behavior is required at all times

Access Policies

  • Membership cards are non-transferable
  • Lost or stolen cards must be reported immediately
  • Replacement cards available for a fee
  • Access is granted only during operating hours
  • 24/7 access available for Elite Fit members only

Children's Access

  • Children under 16 must be supervised by an adult member
  • Kids MMA classes available for ages 5-16
  • Children are not permitted in weight training areas
  • Family Plan memberships include supervised children access
  • All children must follow safety guidelines

General Access

  • Members must check in at reception
  • Guest passes available for purchase
  • Visitors must be accompanied by a member
  • Photography and videography require prior permission
  • Membership suspension may occur for rule violations

GUEST POLICIES

  • Guests must be accompanied by a member at all times
  • Guest passes are available for purchase at reception
  • Maximum 2 guests per member per visit
  • Guests must complete a waiver form before entry
  • Guest access is limited to general facilities only
  • Personal training sessions require separate booking

BEHAVIOR IN OUR CLUBS

  • Respect all members, staff, and equipment
  • Use appropriate language and maintain professional conduct
  • No harassment, discrimination, or inappropriate behavior tolerated
  • Report any concerns to staff immediately
  • Violations may result in membership termination
  • We maintain a positive, supportive environment for all

USE OF EQUIPMENT

  • Return all equipment to designated storage areas after use
  • Wipe down equipment after each use with provided sanitizer
  • Report damaged or malfunctioning equipment to staff
  • Do not remove equipment from designated areas
  • Respect time limits on cardio equipment during peak hours
  • Proper form and safety techniques are required

SAFETY

  • Always use spotters when lifting heavy weights
  • Wear appropriate athletic footwear at all times
  • Follow all posted safety guidelines and instructions
  • Report accidents or injuries immediately to staff
  • Emergency exits and first aid stations are clearly marked
  • Consult with trainers before attempting new exercises

USE OF STUDIO

  • Studio classes require advance booking
  • Arrive 5 minutes before class start time
  • Late entry may not be permitted
  • Bring your own mat for yoga and pilates classes
  • Respect the instructor and fellow participants
  • Cancel bookings at least 2 hours in advance

PERSONAL TRAINING

  • Personal training sessions must be booked in advance
  • Cancellations require 24-hour notice
  • Packages are non-refundable but transferable
  • Trainers are certified professionals
  • Follow trainer instructions for optimal results
  • Premium and Elite memberships include training sessions
