# How to Get Files from Vercel

## Option 1: Vercel Dashboard
1. Go to your Vercel dashboard
2. Click on your project
3. Go to "Deployments" tab
4. Click on latest deployment
5. Look for "Source" or "Download" option

## Option 2: Share Vercel URL
Just give me your Vercel URL (like: https://your-site.vercel.app)
I can inspect it and rebuild from there

## Option 3: Copy Files
Copy the HTML/CSS/JS from Vercel and paste here
