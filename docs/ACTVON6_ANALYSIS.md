## 🎯 TOP MIMIC OPPORTUNITIES FOR YOUR FOREVER FIT WEBSITE:

### **1. EXPAND FEATURE CARDS** ⭐ HIGH PRIORITY
**What:** Add more feature cards (like ACTVon6's 8-9 cards)
**Where:** Enhance your About section
**Example:** Add cards for "24/7 Access", "Parking", "Locker Rooms", "Wi-Fi", etc.

### **2. CREATE ZONE CARDS** ⭐ HIGH PRIORITY
**What:** Visual cards showing different gym zones/areas
**Where:** Could replace or enhance Gallery section
**Example:** "Weight Training Zone", "Cardio Zone", "MMA Zone", "Group Class Studio"

### **3. EXPAND MEMBERSHIP TIERS** ⭐ HIGH PRIORITY
**What:** Add more membership options (Student, Family, Annual, etc.)
**Where:** Enhance your Join section
**Example:** Add "Student Fit", "Family Plan", "Annual Contract" options

### **4. ADD FACILITIES SECTION** ⭐ MEDIUM PRIORITY
**What:** Dedicated section showcasing gym facilities
**Where:** New section or enhance Gallery
**Example:** Show different areas of your gym with descriptions

### **5. ADD CLASS SCHEDULE/TIMETABLE** ⭐ MEDIUM PRIORITY
**What:** Detailed class schedule table
**Where:** New section or enhance Events
**Example:** Show weekly schedule with classes, times, instructors

### **6. EXPAND TRAINER SPECIALTIES** ⭐ LOW PRIORITY
**What:** More detailed trainer information
**Where:** Enhance Trainers section
**Example:** Add more specialties per trainer (like ACTVon6 does)
